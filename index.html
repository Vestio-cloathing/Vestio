KARMA

VESTIO — What You Give

Karma is not punishment. It is not reward. It is simply the universe holding a mirror to what you put into it.

The evil eye at the centre of this tee is one of humanity's oldest symbols — protection, awareness, the quiet understanding that we are all watched, all connected. Twelve seed forms orbit it, one for every cycle of giving, every act returned. The golden circle binds them: nothing escapes the loop.

We built this design not as decoration but as a reminder you can wear every day. Be intentional. Be good. The eye is open.

The Brand

The VESTIO
Story

Where the name comes from

VESTIO comes from the Latin vestire — to clothe, to dress, to cover. But for us it holds a second meaning: to invest. To invest in what you put on your body, in what you stand for, in the quiet statement that every garment makes before you even open your mouth.

Why we exist

We started VESTIO because we were tired of choosing between fashion and meaning. The industry moves fast, prints loud, and discards faster. We wanted to make the opposite — clothes that move slowly, speak quietly, and last long enough to become a part of who you are.

Every VESTIO piece begins with a question: what is the most essential version of this garment? We remove everything that isn't the answer.

How we make things

Small batches. Natural fibres. Artisans who are paid fairly and given time to do things right. We believe that the people who make your clothes leave something in the fabric — so we make sure that something is good.

Our drops are numbered and limited. When they are gone, they are gone. We do not restock. We move forward.

What KARMA means to us

KARMA is our first drop because it is our philosophy, compressed into a graphic. The eye that sees all. The seeds that orbit in return. The truth that what you give — in care, in craft, in intention — always comes back. We built VESTIO on this. We thought you should be able to wear it.

Our Philosophy

Designed to
last beyond

VESTIO is built on a single conviction: a garment should never compete with the person wearing it. We remove until nothing remains but form, fit, and a fabric worth keeping.

Every piece is produced in small batches using natural, low-impact textiles. We do not follow trends. We follow proportion.
